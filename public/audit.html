      How the score is calculated

      The sheet's formulas, and what this dashboard does with each

      Final score (out of 100) = audit average × 80% + rating trend (10) + OptiPulse ÷ 10 (10). Outlets are ranked separately as Cloud Kitchen and PO. For the month the Audit Summary is set to, the dashboard shows the Summary's numbers exactly as the sheet shows them, including numbers typed over a formula. It also recomputes every score from the raw entries and lists any Summary cell that disagrees under Data health. Other months come from the raw entries.

      Tab · cellSheet formulaMeaningDashboard

      Ratings · F, G, JVLOOKUP(ResID, Sheet11, 9), typed, typedEnd / start rating and the 10/5/0 score; G and J are typed by hand.Read from Sheet11 and Sheet6 by date, not by column number; typed scores that don't match are flagged.
